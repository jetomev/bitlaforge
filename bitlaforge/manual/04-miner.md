# Miner

Settings ▸ **Miner**.

bitlaForge mines **Bitcoin only** (the sha256d method). Other coins' methods aren't offered.

## Cores to use

How many processor cores mine. The presets are one, a quarter, half, all but one, and all. **All but one** is the default: the computer stays responsive for everything else.

## Priority

- **Gentle**: every other program goes first. The computer feels the same while mining; the miner gets what's left. Recommended.
- **Normal**: the miner competes equally with your programs. A little faster, and you may feel it.

Changes to these apply the next time mining starts. When you save while mining, the review says so.
