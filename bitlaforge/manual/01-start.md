# Welcome to bitlaForge

bitlaForge lets this computer take part in Bitcoin mining **as the lottery it is**: your processor tries numbers, and if one of them completes a block, the block's reward (about 3 bitcoin) goes to your wallet. The chance is tiny, and bitlaForge shows you exactly how tiny. It's for fun and curiosity, not income.

## The four screens

- **1 Dashboard**: is it mining, how fast, is it safe, what are my chances.
- **2 Settings**: the pool, your wallet, how many cores, priority, heat and the odds.
- **3 Log**: what the miner says, in plain words where it matters.
- **4 History**: every session you've mined, kept between runs.

## Your first time

1. Install the miner if the Dashboard asks for it (Help ▸ Manual ▸ *Installing the miner*).
2. Press **2**, type or paste your Bitcoin address in **Wallet**, and press **F10** to save.
3. Press **1**, then **T** to test the miner (10 seconds, one core, nothing is sent anywhere).
4. Press **M** to start mining. Press **M** again to stop.

## Three promises

- bitlaForge **never starts mining on its own**. Only **M** (or the Start button) does.
- **Closing bitlaForge stops the miner.** If it's running, bitlaForge asks first. Even if bitlaForge were to crash, the system itself stops the miner with it.
- **Your settings are never lost to a save**: every save is reviewed, backed up first, and written in one step.
